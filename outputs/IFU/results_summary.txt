RESULTS SUMMARY - IFU scoring matrix
====================================================================================================

Total scores
Total scores across the 25 systems ranged from 55 to 72 (mean 69.2, median 72, interquartile range 68-72). The lowest totals were observed for SetPoint Medical SetPoint System (55); Boston Scientific Vercise Deep Brain Stimulation System (62); NeuroPace RNS System (62); Mainstay Medical ReActiv8 Neurostimulation System (63); Biotronik Prospera SCS System (66), and the highest for Boston Scientific (Nalu) Neurostimulation System (72); Medtronic Intellis (72); Boston Scientific (Valencia) eCoin Peripheral Neurostimulator (72).

Domain means and medians
Data collection purpose (mean 2.83, median 3.00) and data security (mean 2.87, median 3.00) showed the lowest disclosure concern, whereas transparency (mean 2.93, median 3.00), breach notification (mean 2.92, median 3.00) and data minimization (mean 2.92, median 3.00) showed the highest.

Highest-concern criteria
The most common high-concern items were Transparency Reporting (scored 3 in 25 of 25 systems; total 75 of 75); Control Over Targeted Advertising (scored 3 in 25 of 25 systems; total 75 of 75); User Notification About Third-Party Requests (scored 3 in 24 of 25 systems; total 74 of 75); Privacy by Default (scored 3 in 24 of 25 systems; total 74 of 75).
