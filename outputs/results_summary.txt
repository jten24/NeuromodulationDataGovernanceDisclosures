RESULTS SUMMARY - Company Policy scoring matrix
====================================================================================================

Total scores
Total scores across the 25 systems ranged from 48 to 67 (mean 53.9, median 53, interquartile range 52-55). The lowest totals were observed for Boston Scientific Vercise Deep Brain Stimulation System (48); SetPoint Medical SetPoint System (49); NeuroPace RNS System (49); Biotronik Prospera SCS System (49); Enterra Medical Enterra Therapy (49), and the highest for Boston Scientific (Valencia) eCoin Peripheral Neurostimulator (67); Synapse Biomedical NeuRx Diaphragm Pacing System (61); Neuros Medical Altius Nerve Stimulation System (61).

Domain means and medians
Third-party data sharing (mean 1.36, median 1.00) and data collection purpose (mean 1.66, median 1.60) showed the lowest disclosure concern, whereas data security (mean 2.76, median 2.83), transparency (mean 2.76, median 2.75) and breach notification (mean 2.64, median 3.00) showed the highest.

Highest-concern criteria
The most common high-concern items were Security Over Time (scored 3 in 25 of 25 systems; total 75 of 75); Privacy by Default (scored 3 in 24 of 25 systems; total 74 of 75); Vulnerability Disclosure Program (scored 3 in 24 of 25 systems; total 74 of 75); Transparency Reporting (scored 3 in 22 of 25 systems; total 72 of 75).
